USE CASE SCENARIO OF “WeAreCars” 
Booking Process 
 
Use Case ID: UC-1 
Use Case Name: REGISTER CAR BOOKING 
Last Updated By: Ayoush Chhetri 
Last Revision Date: 16 September 2026 
Actors: Primary Actor: Staff Member Secondary Actor: Customer 
 
 
 
Description: 
A staff member enters the customer's 
information and chooses the necessary rental 
options to make a new automobile rental 
booking. The system saves the reservation, 
computes the total cost, and verifies the 
information. This use case is a component of the 
stand-alone Windows Forms-based software 
solution being created for the Sunderland-based 
car rental business WeAreCars. 
 
 
 
Trigger: 
1. Using the preset staff credentials (Username: 
sta001, Password: givemethekeys123), the 
employee successfully logs into the system.  
2. To make a new reservation for a car rental, the 
employee chooses the "New Booking" option. 
 
 
 
 
 
Precondition: 
1. The system is operational.  
2. The fixed username (sta001) and password 
(givemethekeys123) are used by the staff 
member to log in.  
3. The client gives the necessary information.  
4. The client's driver's license is current.  
5. The client is of legal age to rent a car (age 
requirement met).  
6. To verify vehicle availability, the list of 
currently rented cars is accessible. 
 
 
Post Conditions: 
The chosen vehicle is marked as hired, the 
booking is successfully saved in the system, the 
total rental amount is computed, and the booking 
information are accessible for further use. 
 
 
 
 
Normal Flow: 
1. A staff member accesses the list of cars that 
are currently rented by logging in.  
2. A staff member initiates a new reservation.  
3. Inputs the customer's required information: 
age*, address*, first name*, and last name.  
4. Verifies that the client has a current driver's 
license (required; if not, the reservation cannot 
be made). 
5.Enters the number of rental days* 

=== PAGE 3 ===
 (mandatory, between 1 and 28), charged at £25 
per day. 
6. Chooses the required automobile type: SUV 
(+£65), sports car (+£75), family car (+£50), or 
city car (free).  
7. Chooses the fuel type* (required): gasoline 
(free), diesel (free), hybrid (+£30), or full electric 
(+£50).  
8. You can choose extras like breakdown 
coverage (+£2 per day) and/or unlimited miles 
(+£10 per day).  
9. The system verifies that all required fields 
have been filled out accurately.  
10. The automobile type, fuel type, number of 
days, and any optional extras chosen are all 
taken into account by the system when 
calculating the overall rental cost.  
11. A staff person examines and verifies the 
reservation.  
12. The booking is saved by the system, which 
also refreshes the list of cars that are currently 
rented. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Alternative Flows: 
1. The reservation cannot be made if the client 
does not possess a current driver's license.  
2. If not needed, optional extras like unlimited 
miles plus £10 per day and breakdown coverage 
plus £2 per day can be left unchecked.  
3. The system recalculates the overall cost if a 
booking option (vehicle type, fuel type, rental 
days, or extras) is modified.  
4. The New Booking feature is restricted and 
login is refused if the password or username 
entered does not match the set staff credentials 
(sta001 / givemethekeys123). 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Exceptions: 
1. An error notice is sent and the reservation is 
not saved if a database or system error happens.  
2. If a staff member enters incorrect data, the 
system requests that they fix it.  
3. The reservation cannot be confirmed if the 
chosen vehicle is not available.  
4. The system requests a valid value if the rental 
days fall outside of 1–28.  
5. The system prompts the employee to fill in 
any required fields that are left blank.
